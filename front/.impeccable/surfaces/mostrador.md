# Surface: Mostrador y Productos (panel interno)

Mode: Operate. Audience: owner and employees at the counter, desktop PC primary. Job: find a product fast and register a sale; review products and stock. Must not feel corporate.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The panel speaks like the supplier boxes that arrive at the back room: stencilled codes, kraft, one flexo red for what is urgent. Refuses the white-sidebar-plus-blue SaaS POS.

OWN-WORLD: Kraft rail (#c39a6b) with a 3px ink edge, warm-neutral paper ground, ink #1d1a17, single flexo red #c8102e only for REPONER / SIN STOCK stamps and COBRAR. Stencil display (Big Shoulders Stencil) only for codes, totals and stamps; Barlow / Barlow Condensed for UI. Square corners, 2–3px ink rules, strict module grid.

STORY: The employee types a product name or brand, adds items to the ticket on the right (never beyond stock), picks the client by name or DNI, sees the total huge, and charges. In Productos they scan the catalog, switch to "Stock bajo", and read the supplier order sheet (Pedido al proveedor) grouped by brand.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Rail left (232px) with nav + F-key shortcuts; big search bar top of the work area; product table with padded N.º (idProduct, stencilled), price right-aligned, stock with red stamp when low; ticket column right (420px) with numbered sale, items, giant total and red COBRAR.

FORM: Caja de proveedor (flexo-printed corrugated box), candidate 5 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key 24088dbf. Signature move: stock state stamped like a carton mark (REPONER / SIN STOCK, rotated flexo stamp).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Cited exceptions: solid flexo red also appears on the destructive confirm ("Sí, eliminar"), because irreversible deletion is urgent in the same sense as REPONER. Product codes and rubros were dropped because the backend has no such fields (user-approved); the padded idProduct is the stencilled N.º.
